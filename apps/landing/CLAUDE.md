# CLAUDE.md — apps/landing

Landing page. Owner: Nabil. Issue: #10.

**Update this file in the same PR whenever you change setup, commands, the framework, deploy target, or copy guidelines.** Root rules in `/CLAUDE.md` also apply.

## Design

Use the tokens and rules in `/design` (`design/CLAUDE.md`): Stone & Lime colors, Geist, no gradients on UI elements, mono only for wallet addresses.

The landing design lives in `design/landing.pen` (frame `Landing v0.1`, plus the note `Hero interaction spec`), not in `hellonear.pen`, so landing and app-screen edits never conflict in one file. Its tokens are a copy of `hellonear.pen`'s; when those change, copy them over.

## Setup

Next.js 16 App Router + Tailwind 4, same pinned versions as `apps/fe`. Bun 1.3.9 (`npx -y bun@1.3.9 …` works without a global install).

```sh
bun install
bun run dev        # http://localhost:3100 (apps/fe keeps 3000)
bun run typecheck
bun run build
```

Env (`.env.example`): `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL`, the web app's URL for "Open app" and the Mind cards; `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_VIDEO_URL`, the demo video (default `/demo/demo.mp4`). Default `https://app.hellonear.xyz` (#125); the landing itself will be `https://hellonear.xyz`.

Assets: the favicon set comes from `design/logo/favicon/` (`app/favicon.ico`, `app/icon.svg`, `app/apple-icon.png`). Tokens in `app/globals.css` are copied from `design/landing.pen`. `public/draco/` is three.js's Draco decoder (for the GLB).

## Buttons and cursor (11 Nov)

Every button hovers like Open app (`.hero-btn`, `.btn-swipe`; `.btn-swipe-dark` for a light button that sweeps dark; `.btn-swipe-group` sweeps when its `.group` parent is hovered): a layer of the opposite colour sweeps in from the left and the label flips colour. `.btn-swipe` does not set `position`, so give it `relative` or `absolute`. Every link and button shows the hand cursor (a global rule in `globals.css`).

## Page loader (11 Nov)

`components/PageLoader.tsx`, in `app/layout.tsx`, so it is in the server HTML and nobody sees an empty page. The Mind mark from the logo builds itself on the page colour (rebuilt from HTML pieces, round divs and two small SVGs, so every animation is transform/opacity the compositor runs: it stays smooth while the main thread loads three.js and images; the progress line is CSS too): the head's bubbles (separate circles) pop in one by one, the sprout grows, the eyes open and blink, then it breathes; a lime line shows progress. It lifts once the page has loaded and, on desktop, the 3D Mind is in (`hn:mind-loaded` from `MindStage.tsx`), after at least 1.3 s and at most 8 s; then it sends `hn:reveal` and the Mind's entrance plays in view (the model stays hidden until then). In-page links (nav, footer, hero and CTA buttons) don't scroll through the page: the loader covers it for a moment, the page jumps to the target and the loader lifts. The address stays `hellonear.xyz`, with no "#…" (Ancung, 13 Nov). On desktop, Safety lands on the Tricked cards (section top + 1700 px) and Skills on the Mind Graph title (+ 3150 px), points inside the pinned approvals scroll. Reduced motion: no building or breathing, a short fade.

## Hero (built, 7 Nov)

- Height: the first screen, never less than 900 px (`max(900px, 100svh)`). On screens taller than 900 px the 1440 px stage is scaled up with the height (up to 1.6x, `useStageScale`), so a tall laptop (1800 x 1012) shows the hero full and no page below it; the 3D Mind renders at more device pixels by the same factor so it stays sharp.
- Layout: copy on the left over the sky, a big 3D Mind on the right peeking over a grass slope and gripping the crest. Parallax 2.5D: sky, cumulus, middle hill and foreground (Mind + slope) move at different speeds with the cursor and on scroll (`useParallax` in `components/hero/Hero.tsx`).
- Sky is drawn in code (SVG gradient with colours sampled from the sky art, a warm sun glow top-left, thin cirrus). Sun rays: three wide soft beams from the top-left (`.hero-rays`, screen blend). The front grass is a WebGL canvas (`components/hero/GrassSlope.tsx`) whose blade tips sway, with a gust of light sweeping across.
- The Mind is `public/hero/mind.glb` (`components/hero/MindStage.tsx`), rigged in Blender from the Hunyuan model (scripts in the gitignored `design/hellonear-assets/3d-mv/`: `fix_eyes.py`, `fix_blush.py`, `fix_face.py`, `extract_hands.py`, `build_rig_v4.py`, `render_mobile.py`). Bones: body (squash, stretch, slide), head (turned toward the cursor by the page), a two-joint sprout, two beak lips, and hands. Clips: `enter` (plays once on load), `idle` (loop), `hi` (one shot on a click on the head, then back to idle). Eyes, lids and blinks are driven by the page.
- The hands are 2D images (`grip-*`, `wave-left`), relit to match the 3D light (sun from the left), and moved in step with the clips through `onClip`: in `enter` they rise from behind the hill and grab the crest; in `hi` the screen-left hand waves twice. The rig's 3D hands are hidden. On a fresh load the hands used to rise alone and the body came seconds later (#290): the hands are CSS, which keeps moving while the main thread stalls, and the body's first draw compiled its skinned, morphing shaders then. Now `MindStage` compiles them (`compileAsync`) and draws one frame under the page loader before saying it is loaded, and `start` draws the body once before `play("enter")` starts the hands. Measured in headless Chromium (software GL): the first frame after the loader lifted took 2.4 s before, 0.6 s after; the sprout now peeks at about 0.9 s and the body is up at about 1.3 s, as the entrance means. On a real GPU it is not measured yet (likely cause, not proven).
- Under the buttons, "Built with" and the stack's logos (#290: NEAR, NEAR AI, Meteor, Composio, GLM; `STACK` in `Hero.tsx`, files from `public/brands/powered/`; Meteor and Z.ai show their mark beside the name, as in PoweredBy). The apps are listed lower down, in PoweredBy's "Works with". Phones: the label on its own line and the five logos on one line under it (`--stack-k: 0.72`), checked at 360 and 390 px.
- Phones and tablets (under 1024 px): the copy is centred (title, subtitle, buttons, Built with). The buttons' phone sizes live in `globals.css` (`.hero-m`), not in Tailwind classes: the unlayered `.hero-btn-lg` rules beat Tailwind utilities, which is why the old ones never applied and Meet your Mind's dot touched its edge on iPhones.
- Phones: no WebGL and no parallax; the Mind is `mobile-back.webp` (rendered from the same rig) with the same 2D hands.
- Light on the Mind comes from the left (the sun), with a low fill so the right side reads as shadow.
- Reduced motion: no parallax, no wind, no rays animation, no cursor follow.

## Responsive (10 Nov)

12 Nov, from a friend's test on Windows and a laptop:
- No sideways scroll anywhere. With a classic scrollbar (Windows) the page scrolled sideways by 17 px on every desktop width: the page loader locks scrolling (`overflow: hidden` on html), so the scrollbar was gone while GSAP measured the approvals pin, and the pin kept that 17 px wider width. Fixed with `scrollbar-gutter: stable` on html, a `ScrollTrigger.refresh()` when the loader lifts (`hn:reveal`), and `overflow-x: clip` on body as a backstop. Check it with a scrollbar that takes room: Playwright with `ignoreDefaultArgs: ["--hide-scrollbars"]` and a 17 px `::-webkit-scrollbar` (headless hides scrollbars, so a plain run never shows it).
- Scrollbars look like apps/fe's: a thin rounded thumb, no arrows, no track. The page's own bar is hidden (`scrollbar-width: none` and `html::-webkit-scrollbar`) and `components/OverlayScrollbar.tsx` draws a thin thumb over the page instead (Ancung, 12 Nov: a native bar's gutter always shows the page's background, a light strip beside the sky). It shows while scrolling or on hover at the edge, fades after 0.9 s, can be dragged, and is dark with a light edge so it reads on the sky and the night. With no bar there is no width to reserve, so `scrollbar-gutter: stable` is gone; checked with a real 17 px scrollbar at 1024, 1512 and 1920 px: no bar width, no sideways scroll.
- A refresh starts at the hero: an inline script in `app/layout.tsx` turns off scroll restoration and drops a section hash before the page paints (Ancung, 12 Nov). Checked: a reload after scrolling and a fresh load of `/#safety` both land at the top.
- Link previews: `app/opengraph-image.jpg` and `twitter-image.jpg` (the hero at 1200 x 630: Ancung's own screenshot of the desktop hero, picked 13 Nov, cropped to 1.9:1 from the top and scaled down; with alt text files), `metadataBase` `https://hellonear.xyz`, and a large-image Twitter card. Rebuild the image if the hero changes.
- A light 1 px line across the Mind Graph at the bottom of the approvals scroll on scaled screens (reproduced at 175%, 1279 x 661): the graph's night now starts 2 px above that edge, so the two overlap.
- Every stacked layout (under 1024 px) centres its title and subtitle: hero, approvals, CTA (with its buttons) and the footer's logo and line.

Checked at 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440, 1920 and 2560 px wide (Playwright, every section), no sideways scroll at any of them. The desktop layout starts at **1024 px** (Tailwind `lg`), not 768: below it the hero is the phone hero, the approvals section is copy then a plain card (`ApprovalVisual`: Penny's message and the approval card on light glass, `.glass-lite`, over a patch of the lawn, `.lawn-box`, `public/scenes/lawn-card.webp` 76 KB; no pin, no photo track, no clouds) with the separate Tricked section (its inner boxes use the same lawn and light glass), the Mind Graph section keeps its own title, the Minds cards are one centred column, the CTA stacks the Mind under the copy, and the footer text sits above the meadow. From 1024 to 1439 px the hero's 1440 px stage slides left only as far as keeps the copy 40 px in (the Mind is cut by the right edge). The hero's two buttons stay side by side and the same width on every phone (smaller text and dot under 380 px), and "Works with" is one line. Under 420 px the nav logo and Open app get smaller. Under 900 px the nav has no middle links, so its grid is two columns (logo left, Open app right).


Measured on the production build (`bun run build && bun x next start`), Chrome at 1512 x 805: the longest task at load went from 1.9 s to about 60 ms, the first load from 5.2 MB to 4.4 MB (desktop) and 2.1 MB on phones, no long tasks while scrolling. Keep it that way:
- three.js and the 3D Mind load on their own (`next/dynamic` in `Hero.tsx`), only on screens wide enough for them; phones never fetch them.
- The Mind Graph settles its layout in a Web Worker (`components/graph/warm.worker.ts`, sharing `graph/world.ts` with the page) once the section is within about 1.5 screens; before, those ticks took seconds of main-thread time on a slow laptop while the page scrolled toward it (falls back to a few ticks per frame if workers fail). It paints its still part (lines, bubbles, Minds, labels) once into an offscreen layer and reuses it until the view, the layout, the hover or Penny's step changes; each frame copies the layer and draws only the torch, the moving dots and the pulse. Avatars and logos are shrunk into small canvases as they load, so the first paint doesn't resize 720 px pictures. Measured on the production build with a 4x slower CPU: main-thread work while scrolling the page fell from 6.1 s to 1.0 s, and with the graph idle from 2.1 s to 0.2 s per 3 s; no frame over 60 ms while scrolling. 24 Minds instead of 46 on machines under 1024 px or with 4 cores or fewer, 14 on phones. (On battery Chrome may cap every page at 30 fps; measure plugged in.)
- Big images come in two sizes with `srcSet` (1x / 2x): the approval scene 2560 / 4096 px, the footer 1600 / 3200 px. Images below the first screen are `loading="lazy"`.
- Logos stay small (OutLayer is a 64 px tall WebP, 12 KB).
- Phones: no `backdrop-filter` (the light glass is translucent white); the hero slope is a 1500 px copy (157 KB); the Mind Graph has 14 Minds, a 1.5 device-pixel canvas and draws every other frame unless touched; the demo video loads nothing until it is on screen and pauses when scrolled past. iPhone 13 emulation with a 4x slower CPU: 566 KB first load, no long tasks while scrolling, the graph at 60 fps.
- Mind Graph on touch screens: a tap opens a card and anything else scrolls the page; "Explore" turns on dragging bubbles and the view with one finger and pinch-zoom with two (`touch-action: none`), until "Done" or the section leaves the screen.

## Copy guidelines

- Problem: AI agents that hold money have no limits you can trust. Solution: a Mind whose wallet limits are stored on-chain and enforced in a TEE, with private, verifiable AI.
- Never claim "rejected on-chain"; say "policy stored on-chain, enforced in a TEE before signing".
- Only call inference "private" for NEAR AI Confidential TEE models.
- Money claims follow the #55 decisions: every payment asks the owner first; the per-payment limit and the address list are hard stops; there is no daily limit and no per-skill wallet (one wallet per Mind).
- Connected apps on the page: Gmail, Meteor Wallet and Google Calendar. Calendar is in the final product (#57: backend merged in #62, engine tools in #65); if #65 does not merge before submission, put "soon" back. Brand icons are in `public/brands/` (Gmail from the design file, Meteor and Calendar from their own sites).
- Do not copy another product's characters, names, or visual style.
- English only.
