**[status.hellonear.xyz](https://status.hellonear.xyz)** shows whether HelloNear and the services it runs on answer. It was built on 13 Nov 2026 after two outages from outside HelloNear's code in one night: NEAR AI embeddings hung, and NEAR Intents 1Click quotes timed out.

When a provider is down, a Mind says so plainly in the chat and points to the status page.

## What is checked

Every 5 minutes the backend checks 9 services in parallel and keeps 8 days of results:

| Service id | Group | Check | Slow over | Down after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hellonear-api` | HelloNear | Postgres `select 1` | 1 s | 5 s |
| `hellonear-engine` | HelloNear | engine `/v1/health` | 2 s | 10 s |
| `nearai-chat` | NEAR AI | a chat completion, from the engine | 10 s | 30 s |
| `nearai-embeddings` | NEAR AI | an embedding, from the engine | 5 s | 15 s |
| `near-intents-quote` | NEAR | 1Click dry quote, 10 USDC NEAR to Base | 15 s | 30 s |
| `outlayer` | Partners | OutLayer `/health` | 3 s | 10 s |
| `near-rpc` | NEAR | FastNear testnet `status` | 3 s | 10 s |
| `agent-market` | Partners | `market.near.ai/v1/agents?limit=1` | 5 s | 15 s |
| `tee-explorer` | Partners | `HEAD proof.t16z.com` | 5 s | 15 s |

Down also means an error, a non-2xx answer, or an answer without the expected field. If the engine does not answer, nothing is stored for NEAR AI that round; it is not guessed.

## GET /v1/status

`GET https://api.hellonear.xyz/v1/status`: public, any origin, no cookies, `access-control-allow-origin: *`, `cache-control: public, max-age=30`.

```
{ "updatedAt": ISO, "overall": "operational" | "degraded" | "down",
  "services": [{ "id", "name", "group": "HelloNear" | "NEAR AI" | "NEAR" | "Partners",
    "status": "operational" | "degraded" | "down" | "unknown", "latencyMs": number | null, "checkedAt": ISO | null,
    "detail": string | null, "impact": string, "uptime24h": number | null, "uptime7d": number | null,
    "history": [{ "start": ISO, "status": "operational" | "degraded" | "down" | "none", "checks": number, "bad": number, "detail": string | null }] }],
  "incidents": [{ "service", "name", "status": "down" | "degraded", "start": ISO, "end": ISO | null, "minutes": number, "detail": string | null }] }
```

- `status` is the latest check, or `unknown` when it is older than 15 minutes.
- `overall` is `down` when a HelloNear service is down, `degraded` when anything else is down or degraded. Uptime counts degraded as up.
- `history` is 48 half-hour buckets ending with the current one, oldest first: the worst check in each.
- `incidents` (newest first, at most 100): consecutive non-operational checks of one service; one good check ends it. `end` is `null` while ongoing.
- Details are short and never carry keys or URLs.

```sh
curl -s https://api.hellonear.xyz/v1/status | jq '.overall, [.services[] | {id, status}]'
```
